Resilient Perception in Harsh Visibility Conditions

The Problem
  • Conventional optical vision systems struggle in fog, dust, darkness, and other degraded conditions.
  • Existing solutions can be heavy, bulky, and may still not provide all the information the user needs.
  • When one sensing modality is affected by conditions, the system must compensate to maintain awareness.
  • Result:
    Individual platforms lose critical perception when it matters most.
Our Solution
  • VailSight is a compact vision and sensing system that provides situational awareness in degraded visual environments and additionally detects objects, determines distance, and measures temperature.
  • Maintains reliable environmental awareness when conventional vision is compromised.
  • Multiple sensing modalities work together to provide continuous and reliable perception.
