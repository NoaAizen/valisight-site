The gap

Optical sensors quit exactly when it matters.

Most perception stacks lean on cameras and lidar. Both depend on a clean line of sight and usable light — the first things a real environment takes away.

Darkness

Cameras need photons. At night, in tunnels, or under smoke, the visible-light picture collapses and the target is simply gone.

Smoke & dust

Airborne particulate scatters both visible light and lidar returns. Fire, blowing sand, and rotor wash blind the sensors most systems trust.

Occlusion

A single obstacle between sensor and subject drops the track. The system forgets a person the instant they step behind cover.

The cost

These failures cluster in the moments that matter most. Losing the track at the worst moment is not an edge case — it is the mission.

The approach

Two sensors that fail differently. One that doesn't.

Radar and thermal have almost opposite blind spots. Fuse them and each covers where the other breaks — so the combined perception layer stays reliable when any single sensor is degraded.

By design

No invented pixels in the decision path.

Generative enhancement and frame interpolation can make a display look sharper — and can just as easily draw detail that was never there. In ValiSight, synthetic pixels never enter detection, tracking, or planning. Every decision traces back to real returns from the radar and thermal sensors.
